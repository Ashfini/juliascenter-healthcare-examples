About Us

A volunteer clinic serving uninsured residents of Collin County

Julia’s Center for Healthcare is a nonprofit, 501(c)(3) volunteer-run clinic providing free basic medical care for uninsured adults with non-emergency conditions.

Our Mission

To reflect God’s love by providing quality healthcare, education, and advocacy with dignity and compassion to uninsured and underserved residents of Collin County.

Our Vision

To provide transformational holistic healthcare to underserved communities in Collin County.

Who We Are

Volunteer-driven healthcare with compassion

Julia’s Center for Healthcare is a 100% volunteer-run nonprofit clinic serving uninsured adults in Collin County. Patients are seen by appointment only and receive care at no charge.


The clinic provides chronic medical care, women’s health services, diabetes care, eye exams, and referral-based specialty services through partner organizations.


Founded in February 2016, the clinic continues its mission of serving patients with dignity, compassion, and respect.

Our History

Built on community and service

Julia’s Center was inspired by Julia Grenier, who dedicated her life to serving underserved residents in Collin County.


The clinic opened in 2016 as a continuation of that mission, bringing together volunteers, medical professionals, and community members to provide free healthcare.
